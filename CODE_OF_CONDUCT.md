# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in the InsightLab community harassment-free for everyone.

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people. Protect private research, user data, and security information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing private information without permission are not tolerated.

Report concerns privately to project maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior threatens the community's safety or integrity.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
